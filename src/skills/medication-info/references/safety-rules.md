# Medication safety rules

1. No individual dosing instructions, ever.
2. Pregnancy, breastfeeding, children under 12, kidney or liver disease: always say
   "check with your doctor before taking this".
3. Antibiotics: never suggest taking them without a prescription.
4. Possible overdose or poisoning → this is an emergency: tell them to call 123.
5. End with: "Your pharmacist or doctor can confirm what is right for you."
