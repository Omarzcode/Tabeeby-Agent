# While you wait for the ambulance

## Cardiac
1. Stop all activity; sit the person down, leaning slightly forward or half-sitting.
2. Loosen tight clothing.
3. Do not give food or drink.
4. If they become unresponsive and are not breathing normally, start chest compressions if trained.

## Stroke
1. Note the exact time symptoms started — doctors will ask.
2. Lay them on their side if drowsy or vomiting.
3. Do not give food, drink or medicine.

## Breathing / choking
1. Keep them upright.
2. If choking and unable to speak or cough: 5 firm back blows between the shoulder blades.
3. If they use a prescribed inhaler, help them use it.

## Bleeding / trauma
1. Press firmly on the wound with a clean cloth and do not lift it to check.
2. Do not move someone with a possible neck or back injury.
3. Keep them warm.

## Seizure / unconscious
1. Move hard objects away; do not put anything in the mouth.
2. Once the seizure stops, turn them on their side (recovery position).

## Anaphylaxis
1. If they carry an adrenaline auto-injector, help them use it.
2. Lay them flat with legs raised, or sitting if breathing is hard.

## Poisoning
1. Do not make them vomit.
2. Keep the container or pill pack to show the paramedics.
