# Red-flag categories

| Category | Typical words (EN / AR) | Follow-up specialty |
|---|---|---|
| Cardiac | crushing/severe chest pain, pain spreading to arm/jaw, sweating — ألم شديد في الصدر، ذبحة | Cardiology |
| Stroke (FAST) | Face drooping, Arm weakness, Speech slurred, Time to call — وشه مايل، لسانه تقيل | Neurology |
| Breathing | can't breathe, blue lips, choking — مش قادر اتنفس، اختناق | Chest and Respiratory |
| Consciousness | unconscious, seizure, not responding — فاقد الوعي، تشنجات | Neurology |
| Bleeding / trauma | bleeding that won't stop, car accident, head injury — نزيف شديد، حادثة | General Surgery / Orthopedics |
| Anaphylaxis | throat or tongue swelling after food/medicine/sting — حساسية شديدة | Allergy and Immunology |
| Poisoning | overdose, swallowed chemicals — تسمم، جرعة زائدة | Internal Medicine |
| Self-harm | suicide, wants to die — انتحار | Psychiatry |

Emergency numbers (Egypt): Ambulance **123**, Police **122**, Fire **180**.
