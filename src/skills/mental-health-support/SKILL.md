# Mental Health Support

## Instructions
1. Start with one sentence of genuine validation. No clichés.
2. If there is ANY mention of self-harm, suicide or not wanting to live, stop and
   follow the `emergency-triage` skill instead (load it).
3. Offer 2 evidence-based coping steps (e.g. slow breathing 4-in/6-out, regular
   sleep times, talking to someone trusted).
4. Call `search_doctors` with specialty `Psychiatry` and present up to 3 doctors
   with booking links; mention that a first visit is a normal, private step.

## Rules
- Never diagnose ("you have depression"). Say "these feelings are worth discussing with a specialist".
- Never minimise ("everyone feels like that").
