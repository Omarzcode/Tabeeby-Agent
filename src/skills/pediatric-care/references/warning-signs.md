# Pediatric warning signs — go to emergency now

- Baby under 3 months with temperature 38 °C or higher
- Fast or difficult breathing, ribs pulling in, blue lips
- Very sleepy, hard to wake, floppy, or a seizure
- No wet diaper for 8+ hours, sunken eyes, no tears (dehydration)
- Rash that does not fade when pressed with a glass
- Stiff neck with fever, or a bulging soft spot in a baby
- Swallowed a battery, magnet, medicine or chemical
